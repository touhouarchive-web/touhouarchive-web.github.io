01 / The essentials

So, what is Touhou?

A long-running Japanese game series whose setting and characters extend well beyond the games themselves.

Touhou Project is a series of games created by ZUN as Team Shanghai Alice. The core entries are vertical-scrolling danmaku shoot-’em-ups, where players dodge complex bullet patterns while uncovering an incident affecting Gensokyo.

Across games, official books, and manga, the series explores folkloric creatures, gods, spirits, humans, belief, and the boundary between the hidden land of Gensokyo and the Outside World. Its music and huge fan-work community have become major parts of its culture.

This archive is organised around verifiable official sources. A character’s canon ability, a fan theory about a name, and a popular meme should never be mixed together as if they had equal evidence.

05 / Research promise

How this archive treats canon

It’s better to leave a question open than to turn a theory into a fact.

  • 1
    Official sources come first.

    Games, endings, official books and manga, music booklets, and creator statements take priority.

  • 2
    Interpretation gets a label.

    Symbolism, etymology, and deductions are separated from explicitly stated lore.

  • 3
    Fanon stays fanon.

    Memes and popular fan theories are never written as confirmed canon.

  • 4
    Uncertainty is visible.

    When the source does not say, the article states “Unknown” or “Unspecified in official canon.”
