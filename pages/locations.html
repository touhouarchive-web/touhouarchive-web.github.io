A field guide to the setting

Places of Gensokyo

Gensokyo is a secluded land separated from the Outside World by the Great Hakurei Barrier. Its geography is a story tool: travel, belief, and the boundary between humans and youkai matter.

Landmarks to know

These entries are introductory, not a complete atlas. Use official dialogue and print-work descriptions to establish each location’s inhabitants, relative position, and danger level.

博麗神社 · Shrine

Hakurei Shrine

Reimu’s shrine near Gensokyo’s eastern boundary and closely associated with the Hakurei Barrier. It is an important recurring setting, despite its sparse human visitors.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
紅魔館 · Mansion

Scarlet Devil Mansion

A Western-style mansion near the Misty Lake, home to Remilia Scarlet and her household. The Scarlet Mist Incident makes it central to Touhou 6.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
霧の湖 · Lake

Misty Lake

A lake near the Scarlet Devil Mansion and a recurring setting for fairies, including Cirno. Weather and exact geography are not mapped to modern real-world coordinates.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
魔法の森 · Forest

Forest of Magic

A forest where Marisa lives and where mushrooms and magic are recurring motifs. It is generally presented as difficult or unpleasant for ordinary humans to inhabit.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
紅魔館内部 · Interior

Inside the Scarlet Devil Mansion

The mansion’s interior appears in game stages and related official depictions. Different rooms and stage backgrounds should not be assumed to have a complete, consistent floor plan.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
外の世界 · Barrier

Outside World & the Barrier

The Great Hakurei Barrier separates Gensokyo from the Outside World. Its history is described in official works, but not every detail has a precise calendar date.
