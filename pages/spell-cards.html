Spell Cards & Danmaku

Spell Cards are named attacks presented through the series’ stylized danmaku duels. Record the original Japanese name, official translation where available, appearance, and exact game context.

How to document spell cards

The visual shape of a pattern can change by difficulty, character route, or game. A page should name the version being described instead of treating every appearance as identical.

霊符「夢想封印」 · Dream Seal

Spirit Sign “Dream Seal”

A recurring Reimu spell-card name. English renderings vary between “Dream Seal” and “Fantasy Seal” in fan translations; record the translation used by the relevant official localization.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
恋符「マスタースパーク」 · Master Spark

Love-Coloured Master Spark

An iconic Marisa attack associated with a broad, high-power beam. The precise spell-card name and punctuation should follow the game version under discussion.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
弾幕 · Patterns

Danmaku vocabulary

Terms such as spread, aimed shots, rings, lasers, and aimed volleys describe visual behaviour. They are descriptive labels, not automatically canonical spell names.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
難易度 · Phases

Boss phases & difficulty

The same character may use different attacks across Easy, Normal, Hard, Lunatic, and Extra modes. Stage and difficulty are part of the record, not minor footnotes.

How to read these entries

Each entry is an introductory stub for the wider encyclopedia. The production version of a detailed lore article should add its official first appearance, original Japanese wording, citations to the exact scene or page, and any uncertainty around translations or chronology.
