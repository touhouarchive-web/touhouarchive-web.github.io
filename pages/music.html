Music & Themes

Music is central to Touhou’s identity. Track pages should separate official titles and composer credits from fan arrangements, covers, remixes, and interpretations.

A few famous themes to begin with

Track titles below are established Touhou compositions. Cross-reference the soundtrack for the exact game, stage or character association, and spelling used by a given release.

U.N.オーエンは彼女なのか？ · Flandre

U.N. Owen Was Her?

Associated with Flandre Scarlet in Embodiment of Scarlet Devil. Its unusual title is part of the official track title; interpretations of the phrase should be labelled as interpretations.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
亡き王女の為のセプテット · Remilia

Septette for a Dead Princess

Remilia Scarlet’s theme in Embodiment of Scarlet Devil. The piece is frequently arranged by fans, but arrangements are distinct from ZUN’s original composition.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
上海紅茶館 ～ Chinese Tea · Shanghai Teahouse

Shanghai Teahouse ~ Chinese Tea

A stage theme from Embodiment of Scarlet Devil. Track context belongs to the original soundtrack, even when a later arrangement uses a different tempo or instrumentation.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
少女綺想曲 ～ Dream Battle · Maiden’s Capriccio

Maiden’s Capriccio ~ Dream Battle

A recurring Reimu theme title with versions across releases. Always state which arrangement or version is being discussed.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
恋色マスタースパーク · Master Spark

Love-Coloured Master Spark

An established Marisa theme. Its title appears in variants across the games and arrangements; do not silently merge them into a single recording.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗

How to read these entries

Each entry is an introductory stub for the wider encyclopedia. The production version of a detailed lore article should add its official first appearance, original Japanese wording, citations to the exact scene or page, and any uncertainty around translations or chronology.

Music page checklist

Use these checks when expanding an entry:

  • Exact track title and Japanese title
  • Original game / release
  • Composer credit
  • Character or stage association
  • Official arrangements separated from fan works
  • No unlicensed audio files embedded
