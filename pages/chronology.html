Timeline & Chronology

Touhou’s setting accumulates history through game scenarios, endings, and print works. A reliable timeline records relative order and source first, and avoids inventing exact dates where official material does not give them.

A cautious chronology

The franchise’s release chronology is clearer than the in-universe calendar. Keep those two timelines separate.

1996–1998 releases · PC-98

PC-98 era

Highly Responsive to Prayers began the series’ game releases in 1996, followed by four more PC-98 titles. Some concepts and characters reappear later, though continuity details are not perfectly uniform.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
2002 · Windows

Windows era begins

Embodiment of Scarlet Devil begins the Windows-era mainline sequence and introduces the Scarlet Mist Incident, Reimu and Marisa’s familiar central roles, and the Scarlet Devil Mansion cast.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
Date unspecified · Barrier

The Great Hakurei Barrier

Official descriptions place the barrier’s creation more than a century before the main events. Use the relative dating provided by the cited source; do not manufacture a modern calendar year.

2003 onward releases · Later incidents

Later incidents

Each mainline game presents an incident or unusual situation. Events should be summarized separately by game and route, with any connections to later endings clearly marked.

2000s onward · Print works

Official print-work expansion

Books and manga broaden the setting with journalism, encyclopedic entries, and serialized plots. Their narrators and viewpoints are part of the evidence.

Varies by release · Re-releases

Modern official releases

Later ports and re-releases can introduce remastered artwork, audio, or accessibility features. They should not be confused with the original release date or original game assets.

How to read these entries

Each entry is an introductory stub for the wider encyclopedia. The production version of a detailed lore article should add its official first appearance, original Japanese wording, citations to the exact scene or page, and any uncertainty around translations or chronology.

Chronology standards

Use these checks when expanding an entry:

  • Separate real release dates from in-universe time
  • Record source and route for every event
  • Mark relative or unspecified dates
  • Avoid assigning an incident to a season without a source
  • Account for multiple endings
  • Flag continuity ambiguities explicitly
