Print works worth cross-referencing

Print sources do not all serve the same purpose: some are in-universe journalism, some are reference books, and some are serialized narratives.

東方求聞史紀 · PMiSS

Perfect Memento in Strict Sense

An in-universe compendium written from Hieda no Akyuu’s perspective. It is especially useful for descriptions of characters, species, locations, and how a human chronicler classifies danger.

Image: view source ↗
東方儚月抄 · Silent Sinner in Blue

Silent Sinner in Blue

An official manga arc involving the Moon and a plan connected with Gensokyo’s residents. Record chapter-level events to avoid flattening the story into a vague synopsis.

Image: view source ↗
東方文花帖 · Bohemian Archive

Bohemian Archive in Japanese Red

An official book framed around Aya Shameimaru’s newspaper reports and related material. Its interview-style presentations can reflect the writer’s perspective rather than detached narration.

Image: view source ↗
東方求聞口授 · Symposium

Symposium of Post-mysticism

An official text featuring discussion among religious figures in Gensokyo. Statements should be attributed to the speaker; dialogue is not automatically an objective narrator’s verdict.

Image: view source ↗
東方茨歌仙 · Wild and Horned Hermit

Wild and Horned Hermit

An official manga focusing on Kasen Ibaraki and her interactions with Reimu, Marisa, and the wider setting. Cite the specific chapter for plot claims.

Image: view source ↗
東方鈴奈庵 · Forbidden Scrollery

Forbidden Scrollery

An official manga centred on Kosuzu Motoori, a book collector in the Human Village. It is useful for stories about books, humans, and youkai, but chapter details should be verified.

How to read these entries

Each entry is an introductory stub for the wider encyclopedia. The production version of a detailed lore article should add its official first appearance, original Japanese wording, citations to the exact scene or page, and any uncertainty around translations or chronology.

Citing a print source

Use these checks when expanding an entry:

  • Publication title and volume
  • Chapter or page number when possible
  • Speaker / in-universe narrator
  • Translation edition or translator if relevant
  • Distinguish quoted opinion from setting fact
  • Do not substitute fan summaries for source text
