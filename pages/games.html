Play order and story context

Games & Incidents

Touhou is built around danmaku shoot-’em-ups, with fighting games and other official releases expanding the setting. A title’s gameplay format and its story role should be recorded separately.

A useful starting sequence

There is no single mandatory order for every player. For lore research, start with a game’s story text and endings, then cross-check recurring details in official print works.

東方紅魔郷 · EoSD

Embodiment of Scarlet Devil

Windows-era mainline game 6 (2002). Reimu and Marisa investigate the Scarlet Mist that blankets Gensokyo and travel to the Scarlet Devil Mansion.

東方妖々夢 · PCB

Perfect Cherry Blossom

Mainline game 7 (2003). A persistent winter and the mystery of missing spring lead the protagonists toward the Netherworld and Hakugyokurou.

Image: view source ↗
東方永夜抄 · IN

Imperishable Night

Mainline game 8 (2004). The protagonists investigate an abnormal moon and encounter a story involving night, time, and the Moon’s inhabitants.

Image: view source ↗
東方風神録 · MoF

Mountain of Faith

Mainline game 10 (2007). A new shrine on Youkai Mountain challenges the Hakurei Shrine’s position and introduces the Moriya Shrine’s central figures.

Image: view source ↗
弾幕 · Danmaku

Danmaku & boss patterns

Dodging patterned bullet barrages is the signature gameplay loop. Boss conversations, stage order, and endings can provide story information beyond the visual spectacle.

How to read these entries

Each entry is an introductory stub for the wider encyclopedia. The production version of a detailed lore article should add its official first appearance, original Japanese wording, citations to the exact scene or page, and any uncertainty around translations or chronology.

Reading game pages

Use these checks when expanding an entry:

  • Release year and numbering
  • Playable characters and routes
  • Incident summary per route
  • Bosses and spell cards
  • Ending distinctions
  • Related print-work references
