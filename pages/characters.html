Start with the central figures

The following entries introduce recurring characters and a few figures prominent in Embodiment of Scarlet Devil. Detailed pages should track each character’s exact appearances and spell cards separately.

博麗 霊夢 · Reimu

Reimu Hakurei

Shrine maiden of the Hakurei Shrine. She is a principal protagonist and regularly resolves incidents affecting Gensokyo. Her personality and exact abilities are elaborated across multiple games and print works.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
霧雨 魔理沙 · Marisa

Marisa Kirisame

A human magician and Reimu’s frequent partner and rival. Marisa is known for high-firepower danmaku and for investigating mysteries on her own terms.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
チルノ · Cirno

Cirno

A fairy associated with ice and the Misty Lake. Her first Windows-era appearance is in Embodiment of Scarlet Devil; later works give her a larger role.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
紅 美鈴 · Hong Meiling

Hong Meiling

A gatekeeper associated with the Scarlet Devil Mansion. The games portray her as a martial artist and the mansion’s defender.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
フランドール・スカーレット · Flandre Scarlet

Flandre Scarlet

A vampire who lives in the Scarlet Devil Mansion. Her major early role is as the extra-stage boss of Embodiment of Scarlet Devil. Claims about her private life should be tied to official material.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
紅魔館 · Mansion

The Scarlet Devil Mansion

A Western-style mansion that becomes central during the Scarlet Mist Incident. Its residents form a closely connected group, but their exact family and servant dynamics should be sourced per article.
