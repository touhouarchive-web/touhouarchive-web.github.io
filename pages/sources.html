Source Register

Starting points for checking game history, character details, official books, and the images used across this site.

Sources are not interchangeable; cite the exact work where possible.

Image credits and limits

Images are loaded from their source websites, not bundled into the ZIP. This keeps the download small and avoids duplicating online image files in the package. When the hosted source is unavailable, a corresponding image may not appear.

The Touhou Koumakyou: New Classic images are modern official re-release/promotional visuals. They are captioned as such and should not be mistaken for the original 2002 packaging or for proof of lore. Retailer-hosted book covers are shown only for bibliographic identification.

Important: TouhouArchive is an independent fan project. It is not an official Team Shanghai Alice publication. Rights to characters, games, music, and images remain with their respective owners.

Research checklist

  • Verify a claim in the original game or official publication.
  • Record the game, route, stage, chapter, page, or track.
  • Quote translations carefully and note variants.
  • Separate in-universe claims from objective narration.
  • Use image captions and alt text.
