The archive’s editorial policy

Canon, Sources & Method

A fandom encyclopedia is strongest when readers can see what is explicitly stated, what is a cautious interpretation, and what belongs to fanon. This page sets the rules used across the archive.

Evidence categories

Every article should make the status of a claim visible, especially when names, dates, translations, or relationships differ between sources.

Primary source · Canon

Explicit official canon

Information shown directly in an official game, official print work, official music booklet, or official statement. Cite the work and specific scene, page, track, or endpoint where possible.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
Perspective matters · Viewpoint

In-universe viewpoint

Books such as Perfect Memento in Strict Sense contain an in-universe author’s classifications. Attribute subjective or potentially mistaken statements to that author instead of silently treating them as universal fact.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
Clearly labelled · Analysis

Interpretation

Etymologies, symbolism, thematic parallels, and timeline reconstructions may be helpful, but they must be described as analysis unless confirmed by an official source.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
Not a canon fact · Fanon

Fanon

Memes, popular fanon relationships, fan-created powers, and theories should not appear in factual sentences. A separate fan-culture article can discuss them if it labels them clearly.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
State the limit · Unknown

Unknown or disputed

When the official sources do not establish a fact, write “Unknown” or “Unspecified in official canon.” When translations disagree, identify the source wording and the translation choice.

Image: Touhou Wiki file ↗
Credit the source · Images

Image sourcing

Online images should have descriptive alt text, a nearby source link, and a caption explaining what they depict. Do not imply that promotional art is original game art or that decorative imagery proves a lore claim.

How to read these entries

Each entry is an introductory stub for the wider encyclopedia. The production version of a detailed lore article should add its official first appearance, original Japanese wording, citations to the exact scene or page, and any uncertainty around translations or chronology.

Preferred evidence order

Use these checks when expanding an entry:

  • Official games and in-game text
  • Official books / manga / booklets
  • Official statements by ZUN / Team Shanghai Alice
  • Translation notes and edition differences
  • Established secondary references for navigation
  • Fan discussion only for fan-culture context
