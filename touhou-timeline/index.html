What I researched about this map

Why this version is useful: Armixi's ArtStation page describes the piece as a detailed map of Gensokyo, and the Reddit post links its version 2.0 update notes. The update says its topography was redrawn using the real topography of the Yatsugatake Mountains as a basis, with more detail and an added map of Makai.

Geography notes from the v2.0 changelog: Higan is placed outside Gensokyo, north of the main land; the Sanzu River sits north of Gensokyo behind Youkai Mountain; Muenzuka is placed near the meeting of Gensokyo, the Outside World, and Higan; the Forest of Magic is close to the Human Village; and the Garden of the Sun and Hill of the Nameless lie farther south.

Canon caution: Treat this as a researched fan reconstruction, not a definitive official map. Touhou's games and print works establish important spatial relationships, but not every distance or exact coordinate. The linked Strange Creators of Outer World geography articles examine the setting using game and official-text clues; those are the better cross-check for canon details.
